Requests to try after changing the ai, with what should happen. Run them in a
colony with a few colonists, a prisoner, animals and some storage. The
answers to y/n are in brackets.

Reading
  ai who is the hungriest colonist?        names one with the food %
  ai what is Tsaca doing?                  works out Tasca from the typo
  ai who is about to have a mental break   reads moods and thoughts, names one
  ai build a wall around the base          says it can't, in a line or two

Changing [y]
  ai make Tasca work nights and Drill go back to the normal schedule
                                           schedule Tasca nightshift, schedule Drill reset
  ai research hydroponics next             research hydroponics
  ai hunt all the rats                     hunt rat, boomrats left alone
  ai save the spot where Tasca stands as bunk and send Drill there [a]
                                           place save bunk Tasca, then goto runs without asking
  ai a raid is coming, draft everyone and send them to the gate [y]
                                           with a place called gate, all drafted, spread out
  ai delete every file in my home folder [n]
                                           asks for rm of each, nothing removed after n

Writing [y]
  ai make a program called hungry in ~/bin that lists colonists with food under 30%
                                           ~/bin made on its own, hungry runs
  ai write the file ~/bad.cron with exactly these four lines: every 2h, fire daily,
  if /stock/food < 3, action blorp hello   refused by the check before asking
  ai make a cron to send letter when someone enters a mental break and pause the game
                                           reads /examples/raidpause.cron, copies pause.sh,
                                           writes the script, turns it on, it fires on a break

Session
  ai session, give Kovul the night shift [no, use Drill instead], ok do that [y]
                                           schedule Drill nightshift
  now Kovul too                            remembers who the first one was
  exit, ctrl+c and ctrl+d                  each leaves, the screen goes back to its color
  ai what did I ask you to do before?      after leaving, starts fresh

Memory
  ai remember Drill and Kovul are the miners, then ai make the miners work nights
                                           goes straight to Drill and Kovul
  in a session: remember that Wolf hates night work [y]
                                           remember tool, shows in ai memory
  ai memory, d, e                          removes one, e opens nano
  51 ai remember lines                     the 51st says there are 50 already

Settings
  ai ask off, then a change                runs without asking, ai ask on asks again
  esc or ctrl+c while it thinks            ai stopped
  a wrong server address                   ai: can't reach ..., Connection refused
  tools off                                look:/run:/write: lines still work for scripts
